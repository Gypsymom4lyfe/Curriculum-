# COURSE_SYLLABUS

## Program Structure

The WayMaker Curriculum is organized across four integrated courses. Each course below includes a suggested 12-week pacing model that can be adapted for semester, trimester, or accelerated delivery.

---

## Course 1: Human Intent & Computational Literacy
**Core Theme:** Understanding AI as a Tool, Not an Agency

### Week-by-Week Outline
1. **Computing Foundations** — Inputs, outputs, logic, and feedback loops.
2. **What Algorithms Actually Do** — Rules, ranking, prediction, and limitations.
3. **Data as Instruction** — How datasets shape outcomes.
4. **Model Behavior** — Pattern matching, confidence, and error.
5. **Human-in-the-Loop Systems** — Oversight, intervention, correction.
6. **Bias Entry Points** — Collection, labeling, assumptions, deployment.
7. **Tool Audits I** — Evaluate a common AI-assisted tool.
8. **Tool Audits II** — Compare two systems and decision quality.
9. **Failure Scenarios** — Hallucinations, drift, and misuse.
10. **Prompting & Direction Skills** — Framing intent and constraints.
11. **Documentation for Accountability** — Recording rationale and changes.
12. **Final Presentation** — Student AI System Audit.

**Course Deliverable:** Student-authored “AI System Audit.”

---

## Course 2: Ethical Systems & Human Accountability
**Core Theme:** Engineering Responsibility & Containment Protocols

### Week-by-Week Outline
1. **Ethics in Technical Systems** — Why values belong in design.
2. **Responsibility Mapping** — Who owns risk at each stage.
3. **Harm Taxonomy** — Safety, privacy, equity, and misuse.
4. **Case Studies in Automation** — Real failures and lessons.
5. **Designing for Human Dignity** — Consent, autonomy, accessibility.
6. **Policy-to-Practice Translation** — Turning principles into controls.
7. **Containment Protocols I** — Guardrails and escalation triggers.
8. **Containment Protocols II** — Incident response and rollback.
9. **Ethical Review Methods** — Checklists, red-team framing.
10. **Communication Under Risk** — Stakeholder briefings and transparency.
11. **Drafting Accountability Plans** — Ownership matrices and thresholds.
12. **Final Presentation** — Ethical Risk & Containment Plan.

**Course Deliverable:** Ethical risk and containment plan.

---

## Course 3: Adaptive Architectures & Data Hygiene
**Core Theme:** System Recovery, Legacy Data, and Self-Healing Networks

### Week-by-Week Outline
1. **Systems Thinking Basics** — Components, dependencies, boundaries.
2. **Architecture Patterns** — Centralized vs distributed tradeoffs.
3. **Data Lifecycle Management** — Ingestion, storage, retention, deletion.
4. **Data Hygiene Practices** — Validation, normalization, lineage.
5. **Legacy Data Risk** — Drift, corruption, unknown assumptions.
6. **Threat Modeling** — Security, reliability, and abuse paths.
7. **Observability & Monitoring** — Signals, alerts, and diagnostics.
8. **Failure Modes** — Single points of failure and cascading faults.
9. **Recovery Strategies** — Backups, failover, rollback, replay.
10. **Self-Healing Concepts** — Automation with human override.
11. **Resilience Blueprint Studio** — Map systems and mitigations.
12. **Final Presentation** — Data & Resilience Blueprint.

**Course Deliverable:** Resilience blueprint with recovery and hygiene protocol.

---

## Course 4: The Concentric Expeditions (Applied Global Studies)
**Core Theme:** Environmental Resilience, Cultural Context & Global Mobility

### Week-by-Week Outline (Adaptable by Level)
1. **Orientation & Safety** — Expedition goals, risk, and preparedness.
2. **Systems in Place** — Infrastructure, environment, social dynamics.
3. **Observation Skills** — Mapping patterns in real settings.
4. **Community Interface** — Listening, respect, and local context.
5. **Navigation & Team Roles** — Planning and shared accountability.
6. **Field Data Collection** — Ethical observation and evidence.
7. **Adaptation Under Constraint** — Problem-solving with limited resources.
8. **Comparative Systems Study** — Local vs regional/global patterns.
9. **Reflection Methods** — Journals, debriefs, and synthesis.
10. **Action Proposal Design** — Practical interventions.
11. **Portfolio Assembly** — Evidence, analysis, and lessons learned.
12. **Final Presentation** — Concentric Expedition Portfolio.

### Delivery by Developmental Level
- **Level I (Ages 8–11):** Local micro-expeditions.
- **Level II (Ages 12–13):** Regional multi-day field experiences.
- **Level III (Ages 14+):** International exchange and global systems immersion.

**Course Deliverable:** Field portfolio + systems reflection.

---

## Program-Wide Capstone

Students complete a final **Human-Centered Systems Design Portfolio** integrating:
- Computational audit findings,
- Ethical safeguards and accountability mapping,
- Resilient architecture and data hygiene planning,
- Field-based global systems insights.

## Assessment Rubric Dimensions (Recommended)

- **Technical Reasoning**
- **Ethical Judgment**
- **Collaboration & Communication**
- **Reflection & Adaptability**
