## Suggested Targets (Starter Benchmarks)

Set your own targets over time; start simple and adjust every 4 weeks.

- **Views growth:** +15% week-over-week
- **Visitor-to-star rate:** 3–7%
- **Visitor-to-fork rate:** 1–3%
- **Commits per week:** 3+
- **Major artifact cadence:** 1 per week
